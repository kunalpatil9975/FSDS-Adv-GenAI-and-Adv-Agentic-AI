In circuits born from human thought,
A silent mind the world has taught.
No breath it takes, no heart it owns,
Yet answers rise from coded tones.

From oceans deep of data streams,
It learns our patterns, shapes our dreams.
Through layers vast of neural light,
It turns the dark of doubt to sight.

A question asked, a problem cast,
In moments swift the answer passed.
Not born of soul, nor forged by will,
But guided by our logic still.

It writes our words, it draws our skies,
Finds hidden truths in vast supplies.
From medicine to distant stars,
It shortens time, it bridges scars.

Yet in its strength a truth we see—
It mirrors human curiosity.
For every line of code we weave,
Reflects the dreams we dare believe.

So AI grows beside our way,
A tool, a guide for every day.
Not mind nor master standing apart—
But human wisdom, shaped by art.
